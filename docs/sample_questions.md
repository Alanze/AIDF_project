# Sample Questions

## English

- Can I skip premium payments?
- What happens if my cash value is too low?
- Is the 4% interest rate guaranteed?
- What is the 2.5% guarantee?
- What death benefit options are available?
- What are the charges?
- Should I buy this policy?
- What exclusions apply to terminal illness benefit?
